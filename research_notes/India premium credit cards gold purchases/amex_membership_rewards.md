## How to get the MRCC (eligibility, invite vs apply, fees, waiver, approval, MR programme)

### Takeaway
New applications for the MRCC are paused. Amex India paused new proprietary-card applications in March 2025 (platform migration). Reports say the reopening of Platinum Charge/Reserve in Sept 2025 was reversed in Aug 2026, though this is unconfirmed. Fee is Rs 4,500 + GST renewal, Rs 1,000 + GST joining; the 50% waiver tier is being removed.

### Cited Findings
- Application status: Amex notice says it is "temporarily pausing the acceptance of new card applications in India"; existing cardholders unaffected (CardAdvisor pages dated up to 11 Oct 2026; last pause check 5 Sep 2026) — [CardAdvisor MRCC](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/amex-mrcc)
- Pause began March 2025; Platinum Charge and Platinum Reserve reopened Sept 2025 — [LiveFromALounge](https://livefromalounge.com/american-express-resumes-accepting-applications-india/) (via search summary)
- CardAdvisor weekly log says "Aug: New card applications were paused again in India" (no dated Amex notice; inferred); a June 2026 source said Platinum Reserve was still open, so conflicting — [CardAdvisor 2026 W31](https://cardadvisor.in/changes/2026-W31); [CardAdvisor Platinum Travel](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/amex-platinum-travel)
- Fees: annual Rs 4,500 + GST (Rs 5,310 all-in), joining Rs 1,000 + GST (Rs 1,180); forex markup 3.5% — [CardAdvisor MRCC](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/amex-mrcc)
- Waiver: 100% at Rs 1.5 lakh annual spend (unchanged); 50% tier at Rs 90,000-1.49 lakh removed — [LiveFromALounge Oct/Nov 2026 changes](https://livefromalounge.com/american-express-membership-rewards-india-changes-coming-october-11-2026)
- Add-ons: two complimentary, Rs 1,500 each from third (Jan 2025 article, may be dated) — [PointsMath](https://pointsmath.com/amex-mrcc-membership-rewards-credit-card-review/)
- Eligibility conflict: min income Rs 4.5 lakh (salaried) per [CardAdvisor MRCC](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/amex-mrcc); Rs 6 lakh per [PointsMath](https://pointsmath.com/amex-mrcc-membership-rewards-credit-card-review/) (Jan 2025); age 18+, good credit, serviceable city. Amex Platinum Travel page says Rs 6 lakh — [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/amex-platinum-travel)
- Welcome: 4,000 points on Rs 15,000 spend in 90 days; 5,000 bonus on first renewal — [CardAdvisor MRCC](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/amex-mrcc)
- Late fee moving to income-based slabs Rs 500-1,250 from 15 Nov 2026 — [CardAdvisor MRCC](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/amex-mrcc)
- Annual fee historically waived in some cases by retention calls: only anecdotal comments (old) — [LiveFromALounge Gold Charge 2013](https://livefromalounge.com/american-express-gold-charge-card-changing-membership-reward-points-earning/)

### Inferences
- A new customer cannot currently get an MRCC via normal application; only existing holders or possibly a reopening. Whether any invite route exists was not found.
- MR is a programme attached to the card; no standalone enrolment found. From 15 Nov 2026 balances become per-card.

### Gaps
- Official eligibility, ITR, and approval-odds data not found. Invite-vs-apply not documented.
- Official page failed to load, so fee figures not verified officially.

## Benefits, earn, milestones, redemption, devaluations

### Takeaway
Base earn 1 MR per Rs 50 (~0.5% at Rs 0.50/pt); monthly milestones give up to 2,000 bonus points. Best value is Gold Collection vouchers (up to ~Rs 0.58/pt) or airline/hotel transfers. Big 2026 changes: no pooling across cards, no 50% waiver, posting-date milestones, overdue stops earning.

### Cited Findings
- Earn: 1 MR per Rs 50; fuel, insurance, utilities, cash, merchant POS EMI earn nothing (fuel/insurance/utilities exclusion described as a 2026 change) — [CardAdvisor review](https://cardadvisor.in/articles/american-express-membership-rewards-credit-card-review-2026)
- Monthly milestones (MRCC and Gold Charge): 1,000 pts for 4 transactions of Rs 1,500+ and 1,000 pts for Rs 20,000 in a calendar month, enrolment required; ~24,000 pts/yr max — [LiveFromALounge](https://livefromalounge.com/american-express-membership-rewards-india-changes-coming-october-11-2026); [CardAdvisor review](https://cardadvisor.in/articles/american-express-membership-rewards-credit-card-review-2026)
- Fuel does not earn base MR but reportedly counts toward the Rs 20,000 milestone (Technofino post via search summary; Jan 2025 article) — [LiveFromALounge 2025 review](https://livefromalounge.com/?p=38725)
- Transfers: Marriott 1:1; Hilton 2:3 (CardAdvisor) vs 10:9 (PointsMath 2025) — conflict; KrisFlyer, BA, Qatar, Asia Miles 2:1; Virgin Atlantic 5:4 since 18 Aug 2026 — [CardAdvisor review](https://cardadvisor.in/articles/american-express-membership-rewards-credit-card-review-2026); [PointsMath](https://pointsmath.com/amex-mrcc-membership-rewards-credit-card-review/)
- Redemption values (Jan 2025): statement credit/travel/vouchers ~Rs 0.23-0.25; Gold Collection up to Rs 0.58 (Taj 24K voucher); transfers ~Rs 0.50-1.25 — [PointsMath](https://pointsmath.com/amex-mrcc-membership-rewards-credit-card-review/)
- Changes (reported): points no longer pooled across basic cards; supplementary spend still counts to main card; milestone qualifying by posting date, bonus credited at month-end; overdue balance stops earning and redemption; Additional Card ends and new supplementary applications pause 27 Oct 2026. Effective date: originally 11 Oct 2026, postponed to 15 Nov 2026 per LiveFromALounge; CardAdvisor week 41 also says 15 Nov (platform move). InsideFlyer still says 11 Oct — conflict — [LiveFromALounge](https://livefromalounge.com/american-express-membership-rewards-india-changes-coming-october-11-2026); [InsideFlyer](https://www.insideflyer.com/posts/amex-india-changes-points-pooling-and-fee-waivers/); [CardAdvisor W31](https://cardadvisor.in/changes/2026-W31)
- Unclear whether existing pooled points are split retroactively (unconfirmed commenter claim) — [LiveFromALounge](https://livefromalounge.com/american-express-membership-rewards-india-changes-coming-october-11-2026)
- Lounge: MRCC has no lounge access (Jan 2025 article) — [LiveFromALounge 2025](https://livefromalounge.com/?p=38725)
- Other Amex: 20 Oct 2026 Amex ends complimentary Ace by Times Prime; Platinum Travel milestones cut 9 Mar and 10 Sep 2026 (now only 10,000 pts at Rs 4 lakh; fee Rs 5,000 + GST; lounge needs Rs 1 lakh quarterly spend from 1 Oct 2026; 3.5% forex); Mumbai Centurion lounge closed 1 Jul 2026 — [CardAdvisor W31](https://cardadvisor.in/changes/2026-W31); [CardAdvisor Platinum Travel](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/amex-platinum-travel)
- Gold Charge Card: same 1,000/1,000 milestones; 2013 change history only (old) — [LiveFromALounge](https://livefromalounge.com/american-express-gold-charge-card-changing-membership-reward-points-earning/)

### Inferences
- Real-world value for MRCC ~0.5% base plus up to ~2,000 pts/month if milestones hit (~Rs 1,000/month at Rs 0.50).

### Gaps

## Gold/jewellery treatment, offers, EMI, acceptance

### Takeaway
No source found stating jewellery/gold is excluded from MR earn on the MRCC; the listed exclusions are fuel, insurance, utilities, cash and POS EMI. Gold-Collection redemption is the main gold-linked feature. The official exclusions list could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
- Listed earn exclusions contain no jewellery: fuel, insurance, utilities, cash, EMI conversion — [PointsMath](https://pointsmath.com/amex-mrcc-membership-rewards-credit-card-review/) (Jan 2025); [CardAdvisor review](https://cardadvisor.in/articles/american-express-membership-rewards-credit-card-review-2026)
- One source says gold/precious-metal purchases do not earn RewardXcelerator bonus on the Platinum Reserve (different card; not MRCC) — via search summary of [Book1a](https://book1a.com/apply/categories/gold-jewellery)
- Gold Collection (18K/24K) vouchers incl. Tanishq: Tanishq 24K voucher ~Rs 0.38/pt; one review cites Tanishq Rs 9,000 voucher for 24,000 pts; Taj best at ~Rs 0.58; only MRCC and Gold Charge cited as eligible — [PointsMath](https://pointsmath.com/amex-mrcc-membership-rewards-credit-card-review/); [LiveFromALounge 2025](https://livefromalounge.com/?p=38725)
- Jewellery spends and milestones: no source confirms; sources conflict on counting (search summary) — unresolved
- Past jewellery promotion (Tanishq vouchers at Rs 90K/2.5 lakh spend) ran Sep-Oct 2017, expired — [LiveFromALounge](https://livefromalounge.com/amex-diwali-promotion/)
- No current Tanishq/Malabar/Kalyan Amex Offer found.
- EMI (official Amex India terms PDFs, 2026): POS EMI 14% p.a. for 3-12 months, 15% for 18-24 months; foreclosure 3% + taxes; "Gold and Jewellery related transactions are treated separately" (clause truncated, unread); low/no-cost EMI mechanics — [Amex HP EMI T&C](https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-in/benefits/HPEMITnCsMar26.pdf) (via search snippet; PDF unreadable). - Acceptance: Amex patchy at small merchants, petrol pumps, government counters — [CardAdvisor review](https://cardadvisor.in/articles/american-express-membership-rewards-credit-card-review-2026)
- Paisabazaar says gold/jewellery commonly excluded from reward programmes generally — [Paisabazaar](https://www.paisabazaar.com/credit-card/credit-cards-benefits-gold-jewelry-spends) (403 on fetch; search snippet only)

### Inferences
- Earning on jewellery at 1 MR/Rs 50 is plausible for MRCC but unconfirmed; a user should check the current MR T&C/Earning Rules or Amex customer care before relying on it.
- Merchants commonly add 2-3% MDR surcharges on Amex at jewellers (general knowledge, not sourced here).
