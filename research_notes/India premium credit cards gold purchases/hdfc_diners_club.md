# HDFC Bank Diners Club credit card family (India), as of Oct 2026

Method note: Official hdfcbank.com / hdfc.bank.in product pages were fetched but returned only navigation (page truncated / one timed out), so NO official fee, MITC or reward T&C text was read. Official-domain facts below come only from search-result snippets. Almost everything is from aggregators (CardExpress, CardAdvisor, CardInsider, LiveFromALounge, CardTrail), several affiliate-supported. HDFC fee/MITC PDFs and SmartBuy pages were not retrieved.

## Which cards are active and which one means "HDFC Diners Club"

### Takeaway
In practice "HDFC Diners Club" now means the Diners Club Black Metal Edition (super-premium, income-gated, open application, not invite-only). Diners Club Privilege is the mid-tier card still on sale. Premium, Miles and Rewardz appear closed to new issuance (aggregator-only evidence).

### Cited Findings
- Official HDFC listing includes a "Diners Club Black Metal Edition Credit Card" (tagline "If the world is where you belong, Welcome to the Club") with a savings calculator — [HDFC product page](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/credit-cards/diners-club-black-metal-edition-credit-card) (nav text only; no card details loaded)
- One review says the older plastic Diners Club Black is closed to new applicants; new applicants get the Metal Edition; existing holders keep it — [CardExpress](https://cardexpress.in/reviews/hdfc-diners-black) (aggregator, updated July 2026)
- Diners Club Premium appears on HDFC's "old credit cards" page (suggesting discontinued) — [HDFC old cards (search snippet; page redirected, not read)](https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal/pay/cards/credit-cards/old-credit-cards); CardInsider also marks it discontinued — [CardInsider](https://cardinsider.com/hdfc-bank/hdfc-bank-diners-club-premium-credit-card/)
- Miles and Rewardz listed as "new issuance stopped", Privilege and Black Metal "available" — [BuddyLoan](https://www.buddyloan.com/hdfc-diners-club-credit-card) (low-quality aggregator, undated)
- The Black Metal Edition is described as open to applicants meeting the income criteria, no invitation needed — [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/hdfc-diners-black)
- Privilege: joining/renewal Rs 1,000 + GST, renewal waived at Rs 3 lakh prior-year spend; forex 3.5% — [CardInsider Privilege page](https://cardinsider.com/hdfc-bank/hdfc-bank-diners-club-privilege-credit-card/) (undated; forex figure may predate the 2026 changes)
- Newer variants: none found in this research (not confirmed absent). Co-brand "IntelliMiles/InterMiles Diners" appears at [hdfc.bank.in](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/credit-cards/intermiles-diners-club-credit-card.html) (snippet only; status unverified).

### Inferences
- Treat Black Metal as the primary subject; Privilege as secondary. Others are legacy.

### Gaps
- Official confirmation of status for Premium/Miles/Rewardz/InterMiles Diners; any 2025-26 new variant.

## Eligibility, how to get, fees

### Takeaway
Black Metal: Rs 10,000 + GST, waived at Rs 8 lakh annual spend; salaried ~Rs 2.5 lakh/month net, self-employed ITR Rs 21 to 30 lakh (sources conflict). Open application, bank discretion.

### Cited Findings
- Fee Rs 10,000 + taxes; Rs 8 lakh spend in 12 months waives next renewal — official page text quoted in a search summary ([HDFC fees page](https://hdfcbank.com/personal/pay/cards/credit-cards/diners-club-black-metal-edition/fees-and-charges)); corroborated by [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/hdfc-diners-black) (Rs 11,800 incl. GST)
- Official eligibility snippet: age 21–65; self-employed ITR > Rs 21 lakh p.a. — [HDFC eligibility](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/credit-cards/diners-club-black-metal-edition-credit-card/eligibility) (snippet only); contradicted by [CardExpress](https://cardexpress.in/reviews/hdfc-diners-black) and [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/hdfc-diners-black): self-employed ITR > Rs 30 lakh, salaried net > Rs 2.5 lakh/month, age 21–60 salaried; one review adds CIBIL 750+ ([CardExpress alt page](https://cardexpress.in/reviews/hdfc_diners_black))
- Replacement metal card Rs 3,500 per MITC per CardAdvisor — [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/hdfc-diners-black)
- Forex markup 2% + GST — [CardExpress](https://cardexpress.in/reviews/hdfc-diners-black), [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/hdfc-diners-black). Dynamic currency conversion 1.75% from 15 May 2026 (CardAdvisor says it rose from 1%; CardAdvisor change log lists it as a cut for Privilege) — [CardAdvisor W27](https://cardadvisor.in/changes/2026-W27)
- Welcome: ~Rs 2,500 value plus 1-year Club Marriott, Amazon Prime, Swiggy One on Rs 1.5 lakh spend in 90 days — [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/hdfc-diners-black); HDFC FAQ snippet similar (Club Marriott, Amazon Prime, Times Prime) — [HDFC FAQ](https://v.hdfc.bank.in/htdocs/common/diners-club/faq.html)
- Approval is at HDFC's sole discretion — [search summary of HDFC page](https://hdfcbank.com/personal/pay/cards/credit-cards/diners-club-black-metal-edition/fees-and-charges)

### Gaps
- Imperia/Preferred relationship requirement not found; ITR threshold conflict unresolved; application process steps not retrieved.

## Benefits, rewards, lounge, changes 2025-26

### Takeaway
Black Metal: ~3.33% base (5 RP/Rs 150), SmartBuy 10X hotels / 5X flights; Rs 10,000 RP monthly accelerated cap; Rs 4 lakh quarterly milestone gives 10,000 RP; lounge reported unlimited. Privilege was cut in 2026 (lounges spend-gated, base earn reduced).

### Cited Findings
- Black Metal earn: 5 RP/Rs 150; SmartBuy hotels 10X, flights/buses/Myntra 5X, trains and vouchers 3X; SmartBuy travel + shopping share 10,000 RP/month cap; vouchers 3,000 RP/month; max 75,000 RP per statement cycle; weekend standalone dining 2X; no RP on fuel, rent, govt/tax, wallet, EMI conversions — [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/hdfc-diners-black); similar in [CardExpress](https://cardexpress.in/reviews/hdfc-diners-black)
- Milestone Rs 4 lakh per quarter = 10,000 RP — [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/hdfc-diners-black)
- Redemption: SmartBuy flights/hotels ~Rs 1/RP (up to 70% of booking), vouchers up to Rs 0.50, statement credit up to Rs 0.30; points valid 3 years — [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/hdfc-diners-black)
- Lounge: unlimited, 1,300+ lounges, primary and add-on — [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/hdfc-diners-black), [CardExpress](https://cardexpress.in/reviews/hdfc-diners-black) (CardExpress says check visit limits). Also 24 golf rounds/yr, concierge, travel insurance — CardAdvisor. One unverified claim of voucher-based lounges from 10 Jan 2026 — [search summary, TradeBrains-type source](https://tradebrains.in/money/hdfc-bank-revises-regalia-gold-diners-benefits-lounge-access-reward-points-and-other-key-changes-best-alternative-cards/)
- 1 July 2026: brand vouchers (GyFTR/Woohoo) 3X capped 3,000 RP/month within the 10,000 cap — [CardExpress](https://cardexpress.in/reviews/hdfc-diners-black)
- Privilege 2026: lounge spend-gated Rs 60,000/quarter from 1 Jul 2026 (2 domestic + 1 intl per quarter); DCC 1.75% from 15 May 2026 — [CardAdvisor W27](https://cardadvisor.in/changes/2026-W27); base earn 4 RP/Rs 150 to 5 RP/Rs 200 from 15 May 2026 for Regalia Gold and Privilege — [search summary](https://cardadvisor.in/changes/2026-W27) (second-hand; not confirmed on a fetched page)
- Privilege earlier: Aug 2023 overhaul removed weekend dining 2X, fuel waiver, golf, annual memberships milestone, 12 lounge visits; added 5X Swiggy/Zomato (extra 4X capped 2,500 RP/month), Rs 1,500 voucher on Rs 1.5 lakh/quarter, lounge on Rs 15,000 prior-quarter spend — [LiveFromALounge, 2023](https://livefromalounge.com/hdfc-diners-club-privilege-credit-card-set-for-big-devaluation/) (older than 2025)
- Privilege (CardInsider, undated): 4 RP/Rs 200, SmartBuy up to 10X, 50,000 RP monthly cap, lounge needs Rs 60,000 prior quarter — [CardInsider](https://cardinsider.com/hdfc-bank/hdfc-bank-diners-club-privilege-credit-card/)
- DCI acceptance weaker offline (~85% vs Visa/MC per CardExpress), carry backup — [CardExpress](https://cardexpress.in/reviews/hdfc-diners-black)
- Diners Black 2022 cut: bonus capped 7,500/month, 2,500/day — [LiveFromALounge via search snippet](https://livefromalounge.com/hdfc-bank-cards-points-earning-restructured/) (2022)

### Inferences
- CardAdvisor/CardExpress disagree whether Black Metal was "spared" 2026 cuts; its own timeline lists cuts. Verify.

## Gold / jewellery treatment

### Takeaway
No source gave HDFC's official MCC exclusion for 5944/5094 on Diners cards; evidence is conflicting and second-hand. Treat jewellery as "possibly capped/excluded; verify in the card's reward T&C".

### Cited Findings
- CardTrail table lists HDFC Infinia/Diners gold/jewellery as "Capped" with no figure; Infinia "excluded or capped rewards on MCC 5944 at various points"; points to each MITC — [CardTrail, updated 21 Mar 2026](https://cardtrail.in/rewards/credit-card-category-exclusions-india/)
- Community guide (via search summary) lists HDFC Infinia/Regalia/Millennia/Diners Black as NOT excluding MCC 5944 and 5094, but says codes are community-reported and vary — [CardExpress MCC 5944](https://cardexpress.in/mcc-guide/code/5944), [MCC 5094](https://cardexpress.in/mcc-guide/code/5094) (pages not fetched; conflicts with CardTrail)
- HDFC fair-use limits: from 1 Oct 2024 Infinia Tanishq voucher redemption max 50,000 points/quarter; Apple redemption 1 item/quarter — [LiveFromALounge, 2024](https://livefromalounge.com/hdfc-bank-credit-cards-curbs-points-earning/). Earlier (Jan 2023) Tanishq monthly cap applied to Infinia only per search summary — [LiveFromALounge](https://livefromalounge.com/hdfc-bank-enhanced-redemption-august-2024/)
- Same 2024 changes: utilities (MCC 4900) and telecom (4812/4814/4899) points capped at 2,000/month; 1% fee on rent (Rs 3,000 cap) — [LiveFromALounge, 2024](https://livefromalounge.com/hdfc-bank-credit-cards-curbs-points-earning/)
- Fuel RP capped Rs 15,000/month, EMI transactions no RP, wallet loads none — [CardTrail](https://cardtrail.in/rewards/credit-card-category-exclusions-india/)
- 2024 SmartBuy 0.70/point voucher promo excluded Tanishq; 2018 Diwali jewellery promo (33% back) is obsolete — [LiveFromALounge](https://livefromalounge.com/hdfc-bank-enhanced-redemption-august-2024/), [2018](https://livefromalounge.com/hdfc-diwali-offers-2018-jewellery/)
