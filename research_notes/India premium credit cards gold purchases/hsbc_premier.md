## Eligibility: who can hold the card, Premier requirements, how to become Premier

### Takeaway
The card is issued only on an existing HSBC Premier relationship (not open to non-Premier customers), and is valid only while that relationship is valid. Premier needs any one of: quarterly TRB of INR 50 lakh (INR 40 lakh if the account was opened before 1 Jan 2026), INR 3 lakh net monthly salary credit, or a INR 1.15 crore mortgage plus TRB within 12 months. No invite-only path was found.

### Cited Findings
- (Official MITC, Mar 2026) "The HSBC Premier Credit Card is offered based on a HSBC Premier relationship in India. HSBC Premier Credit Card is valid only if the HSBC Premier relationship is valid." HSBC may reclassify the card if criteria are not maintained; issuance is at the bank's sole discretion — [MITC PDF](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/premier_cc_tariff_mitc.pdf)
- (Official MITC) Qualified Premier criteria: TRB at least INR 50,00,000 quarterly (INR 40,00,000 if Premier account opened before 1 Jan 2026) OR salary account with net monthly salary credit INR 3,00,000 OR mortgage at least INR 1.15 crore with TRB INR 50 lakh within 12 months of disbursement (mortgage criterion effective 1 Jan 2026; INR 40 lakh TRB if mortgage held before 1 Jan 2026) — [MITC PDF](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/premier_cc_tariff_mitc.pdf)
- (Official card page) Card page says applicant must be an "existing HSBC qualified Premier customer" — [HSBC Premier card page](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/credit-cards/products/premier/)
- (Official Premier page) Eligibility also includes "International Premier status": already a qualified Premier customer in another country/region. Fees page adds that an active Premier account abroad makes you eligible to open one in India, but India-specific benefits require meeting Indian criteria — [Premier eligibility](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/premier/), [Fees and charges](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/premier/fees-and-charges/)
- (Official) TRB = total balance with HSBC India on the last day of each calendar quarter: avg savings/current and FD balances, higher of market value or net purchase value of investments, life-to-date paid-up premiums on in-force life policies, demat holdings market value, Smart Home/Smart LAP drop-line overdraft avg available balance, and linked family members' Premier balances (spouse, partner, children up to 30) — [Fees and charges](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/premier/fees-and-charges/)
- (Official) Salary route includes HSBC Employee Workplace Solutions salary accounts; shortfall fee does not apply to customers with a corporate salary account with HSBC — [Fees and charges](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/premier/fees-and-charges/)
- (Official) TRB shortfall fee: 0.20% of shortfall per quarter, capped at INR 2,000 plus taxes; waived if TRB rebuilt next quarter; repeated shortfall (2+ quarters) can lead to reclassification to an alternative account with 30 days' notice — [Premier account page](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/accounts/products/premier/), [Fees and charges](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/premier/fees-and-charges/)
- (Official) How to apply for Premier account: via HSBC India app (apply "in minutes"/QR code) or request callback. Page does not list documents or describe a separate upgrade process — [Premier account page](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/accounts/products/premier/). An "HSBC Premier unified upgrade form" and "account reclassification form" PDFs exist on forms.hsbc.co.in (seen in search results only, not read)
- (Official) Family: spouse/partner and children up to 30 can join the Premier family group (HSBC's Premier page earlier also mentioned parents; the account page lists spouse, partner, children to 30) — [Premier account page](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/accounts/products/premier/)
- (Aggregator, Magnify) Age 18-65, Indian residents only, apply at branch not online; up to 3 free add-on cards — [Magnify](https://magnify.club/guides/hsbc-premier-credit-card-review/). CardExpress also says apply at a branch, bank decides — [CardExpress](https://cardexpress.in/cards/hsbc-premier)
- (Aggregator, CardExpress, page dated Jul 2026) Page claims "self-employed: INR 50 lakh avg quarterly TRB" and describes TRB as "INR 40L+" in one place — inconsistent with the 1 Jan 2026 change; treat as stale — [CardExpress](https://cardexpress.in/cards/hsbc-premier)

### Inferences
- Non-Premier customers cannot get this card; the practical route is opening a Premier account (via app/branch) and meeting a criterion. The cheapest-to-prove route for many is INR 50 lakh TRB (INR 40 lakh only for pre-2026 accounts).
- Even if TRB falls short, the card annual fee (INR 20,000) is the main exposure alongside the TRB shortfall fee, so the waiver depends on staying "Qualified Premier".

### Gaps
- NRI eligibility for the credit card: not stated on official pages; Magnify says Indian residents only (aggregator). Not confirmed officially.
- Required documentation list for Premier onboarding: not found.
- Whether any invite-only/RM-nominated path exists: found no evidence; MITC says issuance at bank discretion.
- Conflict on app vs branch application for the card: official page does not say; aggregators say branch.

## Fees

### Takeaway
Joining fee INR 12,000 (+GST) after activation; annual INR 20,000 (+GST) waived for Qualified Premier customers (waiver is relationship-based, not spend-based). Forex markup 0.99%, interest 2.99% a month, 1% rent processing fee, 1% fuel surcharge.

### Cited Findings
- (Official MITC) Joining fee INR 12,000, applicable post activation; annual/renewal fee INR 20,000 waived for Qualified Premier customers; GST applies on all fees (HSN 997113) — [MITC PDF](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/premier_cc_tariff_mitc.pdf)
- (Official MITC) Finance charges 2.99% per month (35.88% p.a.) from date of transaction; the same MITC contrasts 3.75%/month for other HSBC cards — [MITC PDF](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/premier_cc_tariff_mitc.pdf)
- (Official MITC) Cash advance fee 2.5% (min INR 300) plus finance charge from withdrawal date; no grace on cash advances. Cash advance limit 60% of card limit — [MITC PDF](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/premier_cc_tariff_mitc.pdf)
- (Official MITC) Currency conversion charge 0.99% of foreign-currency transaction value (GST extra) — [MITC PDF](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/premier_cc_tariff_mitc.pdf)
- (Official MITC) Rent and related payments: 1% processing fee plus taxes (since 25 Jan 2023). Fuel surcharge 1%; waiver on fuel transactions INR 400-4,000 — [MITC PDF](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/premier_cc_tariff_mitc.pdf)
- (Official MITC) Late fee: 100% of MPD, min INR 250, max INR 1,200 per month; overlimit 2.5% of overlimit or INR 500 (higher) + GST; cheque bounce/SI dishonour INR 350; replacement INR 100; cash payment at branch INR 100; free credit period up to 48 days; MPD = higher of INR 100 or 100% interest/fees/taxes + 100% EMI + higher of past due/overlimit + 1% of billed balance — [MITC PDF](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/premier_cc_tariff_mitc.pdf)
- (Official MITC) Balance transfer: 10.99%-15.99% p.a. across tenures, processing fee 1.5% (min INR 200) — [MITC PDF](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/premier_cc_tariff_mitc.pdf)
- (Official MITC) Tariff changes with at least one month's notice — [MITC PDF](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/premier_cc_tariff_mitc.pdf)
- (Aggregator) Travel with Points flight convenience fee INR 300 domestic / INR 700 international per passenger per leg — [CardExpress](https://cardexpress.in/cards/hsbc-premier)
- (Aggregator, CardInsider, updated 18 Sep 2026) Rewards redemption fee: N/A; spend-based waiver: N/A — [CardInsider](https://cardinsider.com/hsbc-bank/hsbc-premier-mastercard-credit-card/)

### Inferences
- The MITC excerpt does not list separate surcharges for wallet loads or insurance payments; those are handled via reward caps, not fees (see Gold section). Only rent (1%) and fuel (1%) surcharges appear.

### Gaps
- Fee on utilities/insurance/wallet: none found in the MITC excerpt. The full MITC was not accessible (only the excerpt).
- Reward redemption fees: only aggregator claim (N/A); official reward T&C not readable.

No milestone benefits listed.

## Gold and jewellery treatment

### Takeaway
Jewellery (MCC 5944, 5094) earns 3 RP/INR 100 but only on spends up to INR 1,00,000 per month in the capped group, per post-rollback April 2025 rules reported by aggregators. HSBC's own current pages I could read do not list the cap, so it is not officially confirmed. No jeweller-specific offer, no gold surcharge, and no milestone/fee-waiver link found.

### Cited Findings
- (Third-party, Apr 2025, LiveFromALounge) HSBC originally planned from 1 Apr 2025 to give zero points on Jewelry (5944, 5094), fuel, e-wallets (6540), money transfer (4829), real estate (6513), tax (9311), education/government, financial institutions (6010-6012), non-financial institutions (6051), bail & bond (9223). After a rollback, all of these except fuel became capped at INR 1,00,000 per month; utilities (4900) and insurance (6300, 5960) were capped similarly — [LiveFromALounge rollback](https://livefromalounge.com/hsbc-premier-credit-card-devaluation-rolled-back/), [original](https://livefromalounge.com/hsbc-premier-devaluation-april-2025/)
- (Aggregator, CardInsider, updated 18 Sep 2026) Jewelry in the nine capped categories earning 3 RP per INR 100 up to INR 1 lakh per month — [CardInsider](https://cardinsider.com/hsbc-bank/hsbc-premier-mastercard-credit-card/). CardExpress (Jul 2026 page) says the same group is capped at a combined INR 1,00,000 per month and that gold is not named separately — [CardExpress](https://cardexpress.in/cards/hsbc-premier)
- (Aggregator, Magnify) Fuel (5541, 5542, 5983, 5172, 5552) earns nothing; Magnify does not mention gold/jewellery — [Magnify](https://magnify.club/guides/hsbc-premier-credit-card-review/)
- (Official) Official card, rewards pages and MITC excerpt do not mention jewellery, MCC lists, or the INR 1 lakh cap (rewards page: "Caps, MCC lists, jewellery: not mentioned") — [Rewards page](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/credit-cards/products/premier/rewards/)
- (Official) No surcharge on jewellery/gold in the MITC tariff excerpt (only rent 1% and fuel 1%) — [MITC PDF](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/content/dam/hsbc/in/documents/premier_cc_tariff_mitc.pdf)
- Instant EMI transactions earn no points (official) — [Rewards page](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/credit-cards/products/premier/rewards/)
- I found no HSBC Premier Tanishq/Malabar/Kalyan offer — search shows none (only other banks' cards) — [search result context: Paisabazaar gold spend page](https://www.paisabazaar.com/credit-card/credit-cards-benefits-gold-jewelry-spends)

### Inferences
- Likely practical rule: up to INR 1 lakh/month of jewellery (shared with other capped categories if the cap is combined) earns 3 RP/INR 100 (about INR 3,000 value at 1 point = INR 1 on Apple, or ~3 miles per INR 100 on transfers); the excess presumably earns nothing, but the official source for what happens above the cap was not seen.
- Gold coins/bullion may also fall in MCC 5944/5094 or 5933 (pawn) or other MCCs; no source addresses 5933, bullion or coins specifically.
- Since there are no milestones and the fee waiver is relationship-based, gold spends have no effect on milestones or waiver.

### Gaps
- Official confirmation of jewellery cap and whether the INR 1 lakh cap is per category or combined (LiveFromALounge says unclear; CardExpress says combined).
- Treatment of MCC 5933, bullion/coin merchants, and jeweller EMI: not found. Official Rewards T&C unreachable (404).

## Recent changes and devaluations (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
The notable change was April 2025 (new exclusion/cap structure, with rollback to caps), then 1 Jan 2026 (TRB threshold raised to INR 50 lakh for new Premier accounts; mortgage criterion INR 1.15 crore effective 1 Jan 2026). No 2026 devaluation of transfer ratios was found.

### Cited Findings
- (Third-party, Apr 2025) Original April 2025 plan excluded categories incl. jewellery; later rolled back to INR 1 lakh/month cap except fuel; redemptions paused 31 Mar-1 Apr 2025 for new redemption flow — [LiveFromALounge](https://livefromalounge.com/hsbc-premier-credit-card-devaluation-rolled-back/)
- (Official) 1 Jan 2026: TRB threshold INR 50 lakh for new Premier accounts; accounts before keep INR 40 lakh; 1 Oct 2024 mortgage criterion started — [Fees and charges](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/premier/fees-and-charges/)
- (Aggregator, CardExpress Jul 2026) Fuel, education and wallet loads stopped earning in April 2025 (this conflicts with LiveFromALounge, which says education and wallets were moved to the capped group after rollback); Accor transfer partner rumours in April 2026 after Axis removed Accor; HSBC announced an Accor Bonus Points Festival on 23 May 2026 — [CardExpress](https://cardexpress.in/cards/hsbc-premier)
- (Aggregator) Magnify reports no transfer-ratio devaluations — [Magnify](https://magnify.club/guides/hsbc-premier-credit-card-review/)
- Rewards page lists partner list "may be updated from time to time" — [Rewards page](https://www.hsbc.bank.in/credit-cards/products/premier/rewards/)

### Inferences
- The Premier relationship bar is the main tightening in 2026 for new customers; card economics (3 RP/INR 100, 1:1 transfers, 0.99% forex) appear unchanged.

### Gaps
- Official change notices for 2026 (e.g. to rewards or caps) not found; search yielded mostly Singapore HSBC Premier content, which I ignored as non-India.
- CardExpress vs LiveFromALounge conflict on whether education/wallet earn points after April 2025 is unresolved; CardInsider (Sep 2026) lists them in the capped group.
