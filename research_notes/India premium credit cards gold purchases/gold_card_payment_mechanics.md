# Credit-card payments for physical gold, jewellery and bullion in India: cross-cutting mechanics (as of Oct 2026)

Research depth note: about 10 searches/fetches. Most findings are third-party. No official bank T&C or jeweller policy page was read directly. Official card-specific rules for HDFC Diners, HSBC Premier and Amex MR should be verified in each bank's current T&C.

## Merchant side: acceptance, surcharge, caps, tax rules

### Takeaway
Big chains (Tanishq, Malabar, Kalyan, Joyalukkas) are reported to take cards in store. I found no official policy on surcharges or card caps. Small jewellers sometimes add about 2%, which is anecdotal and goes against the usual rule that MDR should not be passed on. Card payments are outside the Section 269ST cash cap. PAN is needed above ₹2 lakh whatever the payment mode.

### Cited Findings
- Aggregator and FAQ pages list Tanishq, Malabar, Kalyan and Joyalukkas as chains that accept cards in store. They say some platforms do not allow card payment for bullion. No jeweller-specific card cap was found; one FAQ says the only limit is the available credit limit. These are secondary sources. — [Wishfin](https://www.wishfin.com/faq/can-gold-jewellery-be-purchased-with-a-credit-card-4027/), [Aditya Birla Capital](https://www.adityabirlacapital.com/abc-of-money/buy-gold-using-credit-card-india)
- No official Malabar or Kalyan surcharge policy was found. A consumer complaint describes PNG Jewellers (a Kalyan, Pune shop, not Kalyan Jewellers) asking for 2% extra on a card payment. A DesiDime commenter says chains like Tanishq and Malabar do not charge extra, while small and mid-size jewellers do. This is an unverified user opinion. — [ConsumerComplaints](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/png-jewellers-extra-2-money-been-charged-from-customer-through-card-payment-c1435536), [DesiDime](https://www.desidime.com/discussions/how-come-jewellers-don-t-charge-2179365)
- A card explainer says merchants in India are not supposed to pass MDR on as a surcharge, and that any charge must be disclosed before the transaction. I could not confirm the RBI text. Typical MDR is quoted at 1–2.5%. — [CardTrail](https://cardtrail.in/india-rules/credit-card-surcharge-india-merchants/)
- Kerala jewellers' representatives have lobbied the government to scrap MDR on large UPI payments, saying it hurts small and medium jewellers. This points to a merchant incentive to prefer cash or NEFT. — [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/indias-gold-jewelers-urge-government-to-scrap-mdr-on-upi/tldr)
- Section 269ST bars receiving ₹2 lakh or more in cash per day, per transaction or per event. The penalty equals the amount received and falls on the receiver (the jeweller). Card and UPI are not covered. One source says the Income-tax Act 2025 renumbers this as Section 186 (penalty at Section 451). I have only this one source for the renumbering. — [IIFL](https://www.iifl.com/blogs/gold-loan/gold-cash-purchase-limit-india)
- PAN under Rule 114B is required for jewellery transactions above ₹2 lakh whatever the payment mode. Form 97 is the declaration where there is no PAN. The IIFL piece is secondary. — [IIFL](https://www.iifl.com/blogs/gold-loan/pan-card-requirement-for-gold-purchase-in-india-above-rs-2-lakh-cash-rule)
- Reports say KYC is mandatory only for jewellery purchases above ₹2 lakh. These are news reports of a Department of Revenue clarification; the date was not verified. — [Madhyamam](https://english.madhyamam.com/business/kyc-not-mandatory-for-purchase-of-jewellery-below-rs-2-lakhs-dor-clarifies-694326)
- Old merchant-funded card offers (2021, so out of date) show how jewellers used to subsidise card spends. Examples: SBI 5% cashback capped at ₹2,500–5,000, ICICI at Kalyan ₹2,500–5,000, and HDFC 2,000 extra points on ₹40,000. Many excluded EMI conversions. — [CardInsider, 29 Oct 2021](https://cardinsider.com/blog/best-credit-card-offers-on-jewellery-shopping-this-dhanteras/)

### Inferences
- A card payment at a large chain probably carries no surcharge, so the jeweller absorbs about 1–2.5% MDR. Any cash, UPI or NEFT discount is therefore unlikely to be big at chains, but it is more likely at local jewellers. I found no documented cash discount.
- Section 206C(1D) TCS applies to cash, so it does not matter for card payments.

### Gaps
- No official Tanishq, Malabar, Kalyan, Joyalukkas, Bhima, Senco, Lalithaa or MMTC-PAMP page on card acceptance, Amex/Diners acceptance, or caps. Amex and Diners acceptance at these chains was not verified.
- The search for TCS 206C(1D) and bullion returned nothing; I did not verify the text.
- RBI rule text on surcharge was not read directly.
- Older Diners/Amex acceptance reports were not found.

## Card side: how banks treat jewellery MCCs (5944, 5094)

### Takeaway
Treatment varies by card and by year. Per third-party sources, HDFC Diners Black does not exclude MCC 5944 or 5094. HSBC Premier moved jewellery under a ₹1 lakh monthly cap rather than excluding it. I found no official Amex India T&C on jewellery.

### Cited Findings
- CardExpress (last checked 10 Oct 2026) lists HDFC Infinia, Regalia, Millennia and Diners Club Black as not excluding MCC 5944. It lists Swiggy HDFC, SBI Cashback, Amazon Pay ICICI and Flipkart Axis as excluding it. It says Magnus, ACE, SBI Elite and ICICI Sapphiro do not exclude it. Amex is not covered. The site says codes are community-reported and can vary by acquirer, city and terminal. No caps are stated. — [CardExpress MCC 5944](https://cardexpress.in/mcc-guide/code/5944)
- The same site lists HDFC Diners Black as not excluding MCC 5094. — [CardExpress MCC 5094](https://cardexpress.in/mcc-guide/code/5094)
- An aggregator says Diners Black Metal earns base points on eligible jewellery, with monthly jewellery caps. The cap amount was not given and the source is not HDFC. — [Book1a](https://book1a.com/apply/categories/gold-jewellery)
- The only HDFC Diners Black T&C found is a 2017 PDF (outdated). It lists exclusions such as fuel, EasyEMI, wallet loads, rent and government spends, and caps on utilities, insurance and grocery. It does not mention jewellery. — [PaisaBazaar-hosted T&C, 2017](https://paisabazaar.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Terms-and-Conditions-related-to-HDFC-Diners-Club-Black-Credit-Card.pdf)
- HSBC India Premier: HSBC first announced that jewellery (5944, 5094) would earn nothing from 1 Apr 2025. It then moved jewellery and most categories under a ₹1,00,000 monthly cap; fuel stays excluded. Spend above the cap earns nothing in that category. The source is a blog dated 22 Feb 2025, and the exact date of HSBC's revised notice is not given. — [LiveFromALounge](https://livefromalounge.com/hsbc-premier-credit-card-devaluation-rolled-back/)
- A review says cards issued from Oct 2025 onwards do not exclude jewellery on Premier, but TravelOne does. It is third-party and conflicts slightly with the cap description above. — [Magnify](https://magnify.club/guides/hsbc-premier-credit-card-review/)
- The HSBC Live+ guide (28 Jul 2026) shows "no cashback" on 5944. That is a different card. — [CardExpress MCC 5944](https://cardexpress.in/mcc-guide/code/5944)
- Amex India: no official jewellery rule found. A Technofino post claims recent T&C updates widened exclusions to precious metals including digital gold. It is a personal post and unverified. A Technofino reply says Tanishq has no exclusion on gold coins and bars for Amex Reward Multiplier. Both are forum claims. — [Technofino](https://technofino.in/community/threads/is-american-express-quietly-devaluing-its-rewards-program-in-india.44338/latest), [Technofino search hit](https://technofino.in/community/goto/post?id=296789)
- A forum thread reports that an Infinia swipe on a Tanishq coin earned no points, and a reply says jewellery is not an HDFC exclusion. The reports conflict. — [Technofino](https://technofino.in/community/goto/post?id=296789)
- SBI PhonePe Black (revised 1 Jul 2026) excludes jewellery from rewards (official SBI Card PDF). It shows the trend of issuers adding jewellery to exclusions. — [SBI Card PDF](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/ekit-tncs/PhonePe-Black-pdf-27Apr2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- The headline reward rate on direct jewellery spend is probably base-rate only, or zero, on all three cards. Direct spend is likely to count towards milestones only where the bank counts the MCC for earn. This is not confirmed anywhere.
- The risk of a later change is real. HSBC proposed a total exclusion in 2025, and SBI has added jewellery to exclusions in 2026.

### Gaps
- No official current T&C for HDFC Diners Black, HSBC Premier India or Amex MR on jewellery MCCs was read.
- Milestone treatment of excluded-MCC spend is not documented anywhere I found.
- Caps for Diners on jewellery are unknown.
- The Amex exclusion claim is unverified.

## Cheaper alternatives: vouchers, marketplace coins, digital gold, 11-month schemes

### Takeaway
SmartBuy Tanishq vouchers are a redemption route for HDFC points, not an earning route. Brand vouchers bought with a card carry caps and may be blocked or excluded. I found no card-earn data on digital gold. The 11-month scheme and Amazon/Tata CLiQ coin questions were not answered.

### Cited Findings
- From 1 Jul 2026, accelerated points on Gyftr and Woohoo vouchers via HDFC SmartBuy are capped monthly. The figure is 3,000 points for Infinia and 1,000 for Millennia, Pixel, MoneyBack and Tata Neu (third-party summaries). After the cap, base-rate points apply. The overall SmartBuy cap is unchanged at 15,000. Diners Black was not mentioned. — [CardExpress via search](https://cardexpress.in/cards/hdfc-infinia), [Magnify](https://magnify.club/guides/hdfc-smartbuy-gift-voucher-points-cap)
- Tanishq voucher redemption through SmartBuy is capped at 50,000 points. Sources disagree on the period: PaisaBazaar-hosted HDFC terms say per calendar month, BankBazaar says ₹50,000/month for Infinia, and CardExpress and Technofino say per quarter. — [PaisaBazaar-hosted Infinia T&C](https://www.paisabazaar.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/HDFC-Infinia-Credit-Card-Rewards-Program_TC.pdf), [BankBazaar](https://www.bankbazaar.com/hdfc-credit-card-reward-points.html), [Technofino](https://technofino.in/community/threads/infinia-tanishq-redemption.34705/post-1004125)
- Infinia revision effective 1 Feb 2026: points can cover up to 70% of a bill for Apple products and Tanishq vouchers. This is from a news report about Infinia only. — [Angel One](https://oga-prod.angelone.in/news/stocks/hdfc-bank-credit-card-reward-points-revised-effective-february-1-2026)
- Technofino users report Gyftr purchases failing (except Amazon Pay vouchers) and no Tanishq vouchers on HDFC's Gyftr portal. Amex Reward Multiplier reportedly excludes gift vouchers. These are anecdotal and of unclear date. — [Technofino search results](https://technofino.in/community/goto/post?id=883037)
- Digital gold: Jar (backed by SafeGold) charges a 2–3% buy/sell spread. SEBI cautioned in Nov 2025 that digital gold is unregulated (secondary source for the press release). Digital-gold UPI purchases reached ₹3,926 crore in Jan 2026. — [Freo](https://freo.money/guides/best-digital-gold-investment-apps/), [IANS](https://ianslive.in/digital-gold-vs-physical-gold-why-sebis-clarity-boosts-consumer-trust--20251118150411)

### Inferences
- The spread alone (2–3%) probably outweighs any card reward on digital gold.
- Voucher-based earn is small in rupee terms because of the monthly caps, so it cannot scale to ₹5 lakh.

### Gaps
- No data found on: Amazon/Tata CLiQ gold coin listings and card offers; Malabar or Tanishq vouchers on Amazon Pay, Pine Labs or Qwikcilver; Axis/ICICI portals; 11-month schemes payable by card; whether card payments on Jar, PhonePe or GPay earn points or carry fees.
- Tanishq voucher listing on SmartBuy and its earn rate were not verified in an official source.

## Risks and traps

### Takeaway
Main risks are policy change, voucher caps or blocks, bills for later exchange, and interest. Few were documented in my sources.

### Cited Findings
- Offers are often void on EMI conversions. — [CardInsider, 2021](https://cardinsider.com/blog/best-credit-card-offers-on-jewellery-shopping-this-dhanteras/)
- HDFC changed Tanishq voucher redemption in Sep 2024 (forum report). — [Technofino search results](https://technofino.in/community/goto/post?id=296789)
- Rewards programme changes and devaluations are frequent: the HSBC 2025 notice, HDFC Jul 2026 voucher caps and SBI Jul 2026 exclusions above.
- Cap size limits: the ₹1 lakh HSBC jewellery cap would apply to a ₹5 lakh purchase.

### Inferences
- A ₹5 lakh single swipe can exceed the HSBC monthly jewellery cap, so splitting across statement cycles or cards may matter. It also raises utilisation and a possible bank fraud or limit review. This is general reasoning and is not sourced.
- For later buyback, the invoice should show full payment by card; no source was found on this.

### Gaps
- No sourced information on gold voucher churning blocks, 1% fees on wallet loads, GST on card fees, chargeback practice, or CIBIL impact. Do not assume details here.

## Quantification: ₹5 lakh purchase

### Takeaway
No cited source gives a net-gain figure. The scenarios below are my arithmetic from the cited cap figures and assumed rates, and they are illustrations only.

### Cited Findings
- HSBC jewellery cap ₹1,00,000 per month. — [LiveFromALounge](https://livefromalounge.com/hsbc-premier-credit-card-devaluation-rolled-back/)
- SmartBuy voucher accelerated points cap: Infinia 3,000 points per month (20 bonus points per ₹150, so cap reached at ₹22,500 of vouchers). — [Magnify](https://magnify.club/guides/hsbc-premier-credit-card-review/) (search summary; see voucher section)

### Inferences
- Direct swipe: if jewellery earns base rate or zero, the gain is about 0–1% (₹0–5,000, using an assumed base rate of up to 1%). If the jeweller adds a 2% surcharge, the net is about -2% (-₹10,000) to -1%.
- Vouchers: with a monthly cap of about ₹22,500 of accelerated voucher spend, the bonus is a small part of ₹5 lakh. Treat any 1–3% voucher figure as unproven at this scale.
- Digital gold: a 2–3% spread means a likely net loss.

### Gaps
- Actual reward rates for the three cards, and the reward value per point, were not collected here; another researcher or the card-specific notes should supply them.
