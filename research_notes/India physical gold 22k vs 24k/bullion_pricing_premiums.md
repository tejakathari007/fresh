## Is 22k bullion priced strictly proportional to purity? How do VA/making/brand premiums differ for 22k vs 24k coins and bars?

### Takeaway
Spot-rate quotes are proportional to purity (22k = 91.67% of 24k), but I found no sourced evidence that 22k coins are sold at the same low premium as 24k bars. Making/VA is a percentage of gold value, so on a per-pure-gram basis 22k coins pay the same or higher premium plus lose 8.3% of weight to alloy. I found no primary-source 22k-vs-24k coin making-charge table; this is a major gap.

### Cited Findings
- Malabar's published rates on 3 Oct 2026: 22k (916) Rs 13,775/g and 24k (999) Rs 15,027/g. The ratio 13,775/15,027 = 0.9167, i.e. exactly proportional to purity. (secondary, via search snippet citing Malabar live rate) - [Malabar live rate page](https://www.malabargoldanddiamonds.com/in/pan-india/en/live-gold-rate.html); see also [inkl 3 Oct 2026 rate roundup](https://www.inkl.com/news/gold-rate-today-october-3-2026-check-24k-22k-20k-18k-gold-jewellery-prices-from-ibja-malabar-gold-diamonds-joyalukkas-kalyan-jewellers-and-tanishq)
- Benchmark (MetalsCost, not a seller) 3 Oct 2026: 24k Rs 14,922.60/g, 22k Rs 13,679.05/g (ratio 0.9167); site states it is an independent benchmark, not Tanishq's rate - [MetalsCost](https://metalscost.com/tanishq-gold-coin)
- Malabar sells both 22k (916) and 24k (999) coins, in 1g, 2g, 5g, 8g, 10g etc., via Amazon.in and Tata CLiQ with sealed packaging - [Tata CLiQ 22k 1g](https://www.tatacliq.com/malabar-gold-and-diamonds-22k-916-1gm-gold-coin/p-mp000000002612435), [Amazon 22k 10g](https://www.amazon.in/Malabar-Gold-Diamonds-Yellow-Coin/dp/B07C9PRR6B), [Amazon 24k](https://www.amazon.in/Malabar-Gold-Diamonds-Purity-MGRS999P5G/dp/B017IUK7T8)

### Inferences
- Because a 22k coin contains 0.9167 g pure gold per gram, its cost per pure gram = (22k rate + VA)/0.9167; at an equal VA percent it is the same premium, but any flat Rs/g VA is spread over less pure gold. Resale of 22k coins from a jeweller is typically at gold-content value, so VA is lost (generic, not verified for a named seller).
- 22k coins exist mainly for jewellery-store gifting/exchange; 24k 999 is the standard investment form.

## BIS hallmarking rules for coins and bars

### Takeaway
Mandatory HUID hallmarking applies to jewellery/artefacts, not to coins and bars; gold bullion is an explicit exemption. BIS standard IS 1417:2016 covers 995 and 999 bars, with voluntary BIS-licensed marking by approved refineries/mints. I found no source supporting a BIS-hallmarked 22k bar.

### Cited Findings
- Gold bullion in bar, plate, sheet, foil, rod, wire, strip, tube or coin form is exempt from the mandatory jewellery hallmarking order - [BIS Hallmarking/Jewellers](https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-jewellers/?lang=en) (via search snippet), [BIS overview](https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/?lang=en)
- Refineries may obtain a BIS licence to mark standard bars per IS 1417:2016 (995 and 999 fineness only); 999/995 bullion hallmarking by BIS-approved refineries/mints only - [BIS Jewellers page](https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-jewellers/?lang=en)
- Jewellery hallmarks are 22K (916), 18K (750), 14K (585) with HUID; 24k (999) stamped on coins/bars separately - [Coinbazaar blog](https://coinbazaar.in/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-bis-hallmarked-jewellery/), [Bhima guide](https://www.bhimagold.com/blogs/why-you-should-never-buy-gold-without-checking)
- Branded coins (MMTC-PAMP) are 999.9 with assay/sealed card packaging (seller claim) - [MMTC-PAMP](https://www.mmtcpamp.com/shop/gold/gold-bar)

### Inferences
- A 22k coin is outside BIS's bullion standard (995/999); its 916 purity rests on the seller's brand and any assay card, not on a BIS bullion licence. Buyer should insist on sealed assay-card packs from mint-licensed sources.

### Gaps
- Could not verify whether a Jan-2026+ BIS amendment changed coin/bar rules; official BIS pages only seen via snippet. No explicit statement on 22k bar legitimacy.

## GST on coins/bars and on making/VA charges

### Takeaway
3% GST on gold value for coins, bars and jewellery irrespective of purity; 5% applies to job-work/making only in the job-work context (jewellery). For coins/bars sold by a seller, VA is generally billed within the supply taxed at 3% (inference).

### Cited Findings
- GST 3% on gold (bars, coins, jewellery); 5% on making charges of ornaments; job work by karigar 5% on job-work value only - [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-impact-on-gold), [IndiaFilings](https://www.indiafilings.com/learn/gst-rate-gold), [IIFL](https://www.iifl.com/blogs/gold-loan/gst-on-gold-in-india-2026-complete-guide-for-buyers-and-sellers)
- Local premium typically 1-3% (generic blog) - [Bajaj Broking](https://www.bajajbroking.in/share-market-news/gold-price-in-india-today)

### Inferences
- Making charges on a jewellery invoice taxed at 5% apply when billed as a separate job-work service; for branded coins, the listing says price is inclusive, consistent with 3% overall. Not confirmed from a CBIC notification.

### Gaps
- Did not retrieve the CBIC notification text; coin VA treatment unconfirmed. Tanishq/MetalsCost mention of "18% GST" (MetalsCost page) is not applicable to bullion and appears erroneous.
