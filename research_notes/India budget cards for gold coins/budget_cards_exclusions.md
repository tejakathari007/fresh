# India budget/mid-range credit cards: gold/jewellery/bullion reward treatment (as of 16 Oct 2026)

Scope note: about 15 tool calls were used. Many issuer PDFs and pages failed to load or parse (listed in Gaps). Only a handful of cards are verified from official documents. The rest rest on aggregators (CardExpress, CardInsider, CardAdvisor, BankBazaar), which are flagged as such. Nothing found specifically covers MCC 5933 or bullion dealers; coin purchases from a jeweller (Tanishq, Malabar) usually code as 5944, and bullion/MMTC-type dealers may code as 5094 or others. Per CardExpress, merchant coding can differ by acquirer, city and app vs POS.

## Which cards exclude or cap jewellery (MCC 5944 / 5094 / 5933), with source and date

### Takeaway
Confirmed from official issuer documents: Swiggy HDFC, PhonePe SBI SELECT BLACK (from 1 Jul 2026) and Axis ACE (per search snippet of the T&C) exclude jewellery; Amazon Pay ICICI and SBI Cashback are excluded per aggregators and snippets. Cards that appear to earn on jewellery per aggregators (SimplyCLICK, Millennia, Regalia, IDFC FIRST Wealth/Classic, AU LIT, Scapia, Tata Neu, IndusInd Tiger) are not confirmed from official PDFs, and the sources conflict on some (Axis ACE, IDFC Classic).

### Cited Findings
- PhonePe SBI Card SELECT BLACK: from 1 Jul 2026, reward points will not accrue on Jewellery (MCC 5094, 5944), plus tolls 4784, gifts 5947, education 8220 and non-PhonePe-app utilities/insurance/UPI. Monthly RP caps also changed (Eligible Online Spends 2,000 to 1,000 RP; PhonePe spends 2,000 to 1,500 plus 500 insurance). Official one-page notice, file dated 27 May 2026 (I read it with pdftotext). — [SBI Card PDF](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/ekit-tncs/PhonePe-Black-pdf-27Apr2026.pdf)
- PhonePe SBI PURPLE: a CardAdvisor page says the same exclusions apply to PURPLE (aggregator, not verified in an official PDF). — [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/cards/sbi-phonepe-purple); see also [Goodreturns](https://www.goodreturns.in/personal-finance/new-reward-point-rules-from-july-1-sbi-card-cuts-benefits-on-phonepe-select-credit-cards-1512767.html)
- Swiggy HDFC (Ornge/Black): the 1% "other spends" category excludes Fuel, Rent, Govt, EMI, Gaming, Education, Gift Card, Wallet and Jewelry (MCC 5094, 5944 listed). The terms PDF also carries a filename "MITC Online Version _ 08.08.23", so the document may date from Aug 2023 (an older document; the bank says the list can change at any time). — [HDFC T&C PDF](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/content/dam/hdfcbankpws/in/en/pdfs/Combined_TnCs_Ornge_and_BLCK.pdf)

- Scapia (Federal): reviews list gold and jewellery as a category earning about 2%, and exclusion lists (fuel, rent, EMI, forex; insurance and utilities removed Feb 2026) do not mention jewellery. Aggregator only. — [Book1a](https://book1a.com/blog/credit-cards/scapia/credit-card-review/); [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/articles/scapia-federal-credit-card-review-2026)

### Inferences
- A buyer who pays a jeweller for a coin with Swiggy HDFC, ACE, Flipkart Axis, Amazon Pay ICICI, SBI Cashback, PhonePe SBI, IndusInd CRED or HSBC Live+ should expect about zero rewards.
- Official sources I read are all MITC-style; aggregator lists conflict, so a small test purchase and statement check is the safest verification for any "does not exclude" card.

## Accelerated cashback on Tanishq/Titan-type merchants and caps

### Takeaway
The clear winners for jeweller spends are brand-linked cards: Titan SBI (3% Tanishq, capped Rs 25,000 per quarter) and Tata Neu Infinity (5% NeuCoins claimed, but conflicting evidence). Tata Neu Plus is 2% on Tanishq per HDFC's own page.

### Cited Findings
- Titan SBI Card: Rs 2,999 + GST joining/annual; 3% value back at Tanishq (capped Rs 25,000 per quarter, i.e. up to Rs 1 lakh a year in vouchers), 5% at CaratLane/Mia/Zoya (Rs 10,000 per quarter cap); rewards are gift vouchers. One CardInsider passage also says "up to 5%" on Tanishq, an inconsistency on the page. — [CardInsider, 2 Jun 2026](https://cardinsider.com/best-credit-cards-jewellery-spends/); [Paisabazaar](https://www.paisabazaar.com/credit-card/credit-cards-benefits-gold-jewelry-spends) (3% quarterly cap, aggregator)
- Tata Neu Infinity HDFC: CardInsider says Rs 1,499 + GST fee, 5% NeuCoins at Tanishq. CardExpress success story (undated; aggregator-style testimonial) says Rs 4.95 lakh at Tanishq earned 24,750 NeuCoins (5%), unlimited on Tata brands, 1.5% on non-Tata spends, NeuCoins expire after one year, fee Rs 3,000 waived at Rs 3 lakh spend. The two sources conflict on the fee (Rs 1,499 vs Rs 3,000); I could not read HDFC's page. — [CardInsider](https://cardinsider.com/best-credit-cards-jewellery-spends/); [CardExpress story](https://cardexpress.in/success-stories/tata-neu-infinity-jewelry-to-flights); [HDFC Infinity page (no terms visible)](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/credit-cards/tata-neu-infinity-hdfc-bank-credit-card)
- Tata Neu Plus HDFC: HDFC's value chart lists Tanishq at 2% NeuCoins and says Tanishq spends are not eligible for the extra 5% NeuPass bonus. — [HDFC Plus value chart](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/credit-cards/tata-neu-plus-hdfc-bank-credit-card/value-chart)
- Anecdotes (forum, not authoritative) report occasional blocked NeuCoins at Tanishq on Tata Neu cards. — [DesiDime](https://www.desidime.com/discussions/dilemma-should-we-use-hdfc-tata-neu-credit-card-at-tanishq-for-jewellery-purchase)
- Reliance SBI Prime: 10 RP per Rs 100 at Reliance Jewels, offline only (Rs 2,999 + GST). — [CardInsider](https://cardinsider.com/best-credit-cards-jewellery-spends/)
- Axis Horizon: 2 EDGE Miles per Rs 100 on jewellery (Rs 3,000 + GST) (page inconsistent about miles vs points). Amex Platinum Travel: 1 MR point per Rs 50 (Rs 5,000 + GST). — [CardInsider](https://cardinsider.com/best-credit-cards-jewellery-spends/)
- SBI's Tanishq e-voucher page says the cashback offer does not apply to Tanishq vouchers. Tanishq gold coin retailer offers (up to 2% cashback, Aug 2026 via CashKaro) are not card rewards. — [SBI Card](https://www.sbicard.com/en/personal/rewards/tanishq-e-voucher-rs-1000); [CashKaro](https://cashkaro.com/stores/tanishq-coupons)

### Inferences
- Titan SBI quarterly cap means a Rs 1 lakh coin earns about Rs 3,000 (3%), but a Rs 3 lakh purchase earns at most Rs 25,000 voucher value per quarter cap-limited, i.e. the cap does not bind at Rs 3 lakh only if the cap is on rewards rather than spend; the wording "Rs 25,000 per quarter" is ambiguous (spend vs reward) and unresolved. If it is a spend cap, Rs 3 lakh would earn 3% on Rs 25,000 only (about Rs 750) plus base on the remainder. Verify.
- Whether Tanishq counts "gold coins" for brand accelerators (versus jewellery) is not documented; some brands exclude coins from offers.

### Gaps
- Whether Titan SBI's cap is spend or reward, and whether coins qualify; no Malabar-specific accelerator card found.

## Top 5 for gold coin purchases (expected reward on Rs 1 lakh and Rs 3 lakh), with uncertainty

### Takeaway
Provisional ranking; none verified end-to-end. Gold coin purchases are likely coded 5944/5094, so do a test swipe first. Realistic reward is 0% on most popular cards.

### Inferences
1. Titan SBI (Tanishq only): 3% = about Rs 3,000 on Rs 1 lakh; Rs 3 lakh depends on the quarterly cap (about Rs 9,000 if cap is on rewards, about Rs 750 or more if on spend). Medium confidence on rate, low on coin eligibility.
2. Tata Neu Infinity HDFC: 5% at Tanishq claimed = Rs 5,000 / Rs 15,000; or 1.5% = Rs 1,500 / Rs 4,500 if coded as non-Tata or Tanishq is blocked. Low-to-medium confidence; HDFC page unread.
3. Scapia: about 2% = Rs 2,000 / Rs 6,000 per aggregators; check any monthly caps; low-medium confidence.
4. Tata Neu Plus HDFC: 2% Tanishq per HDFC value chart = Rs 2,000 / Rs 6,000 (monthly cap not verified).
5. HDFC Millennia/Regalia, IDFC FIRST Wealth, AU LIT or SBI SimplyCLICK (all "not excluded" per CardExpress): reward rate on jewellery unverified; likely base-rate level (roughly 0.5-1%, my assumption, not sourced).

Not recommended for coins: Swiggy HDFC, Axis ACE, Flipkart Axis, Amazon Pay ICICI, SBI Cashback, PhonePe SBI, IndusInd CRED, HSBC Live+.

### Gaps
- No verified reward-rate numbers for the fifth tier; no card verified for a non-jeweller bullion dealer.
