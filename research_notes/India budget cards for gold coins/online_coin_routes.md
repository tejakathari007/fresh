# Online/e-commerce routes to buy physical gold coins in India with a credit card (as of Oct 2026)

Method note: findings come from WebSearch result summaries only (about 10 searches). I did not open or fetch the underlying pages, and no platform product page (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Tata CLiQ, Tanishq, Malabar) was checked directly. Most sources are third-party. Treat every figure as indicative, not verified. Searches targeting Reddit were skipped as instructed.

## Which platforms sell branded coins, at what price/premium, with invoice/authenticity/buyback

### Takeaway
No official live price list was obtained. The only concrete data points are one undated Tanishq product page (making charge about 7.5%), one June 2026 affiliate roundup of Amazon prices, and generic rules of thumb. Sizes, invoice and buyback terms per platform could not be confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Tanishq's own product page for a 10 g 24K Ganesha coin showed a gold rate of Rs 6,304.31/g, making charges of Rs 4,728.23 and a total of Rs 69,804 incl. GST. The page is undated and the rate is far below 2026 prices, so it is a stale snapshot. It shows Tanishq coins carry a making charge of roughly 7.5% of gold value (the search tool's own calculation) — [Tanishq product page (Salesforce staging mirror)](https://bkck-029.dx.commercecloud.salesforce.com/s/Tanishq/10-gram-24-karat-gold-coin-with-ganesha-motif/600107zaaras00.html)
- A June 2026 affiliate roundup lists on Amazon: Malabar 10 g 24K coin Rs 1,67,339; MMTC-PAMP 10 g bar Rs 1,69,220; BHIMA 10 g bar Rs 1,62,799. Items are a mix of coins and bars and the page is an affiliate listing, so verify on Amazon — [MagicDrop, 11 June 2026](https://magicdrop.in/drops/10-gm-gold-bar)
- A blog says Tanishq usually has high making charges, that 3-5% of spot is a reasonable making charge, and that a 10 g coin can cost about 10% over the screen price, with 3% GST on top — [Dalal Street Bulls](https://dalalstreetbulls.substack.com/p/how-are-gold-coins-priced). Blog, not authoritative.
- 3% GST applies to gold coins and digital gold, on gold value plus making charge. The 5% making-charge GST rate is for jewellery, not coins — [Vyapar](https://vyaparapp.in/blog/gst-on-gold/)
- A third-party guide says Tanishq invoices itemise gold weight, rate, making charges and GST, and that pieces are BIS hallmarked and shipped with an invoice. Not confirmed with Tanishq documentation — [Finology Select](https://select.finology.in/articles/credit-card/buy-gold-with-credit-card)
- MMTC-PAMP help page: gold can be sold back via partner platforms during open windows at the rate shown there; sale back is possible 72 hours after first purchase, with a 5 pm same-day price cut-off. This is described for the digital product. MMTC-PAMP appears to offer digital gold through partners rather than its own platform — [MMTC-PAMP help](https://www.mmtcpamp.com/contact). A promotional article claims a lifetime buyback at market rate for physical coins, which conflicts with the official page's conditions — [Patna Press](https://patnapress.com/?p=42601)

### Inferences
- Likely ordering for a 10 g coin: Tanishq (highest making charge) > Malabar/others > MMTC-PAMP/Bhima, but no same-day comparison against the 24K rate exists in my sources.
- For any later exchange or resale, buy only where a GST invoice naming the seller and coin is issued; that is only claimed for Tanishq here.

### Gaps
- Not obtained: same-day 24K rate vs each seller's price; coin sizes by platform; GST invoice and authenticity guarantees for Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Tata CLiQ, Kalyan, Joyalukkas, CaratLane, Senco; Tata CLiQ/Myntra/Flipkart coin listings; bank portals' (Axis Edge, ICICI iShop, SBI Rewards) coin listings.
- Return policy on coins per platform: not found.

## Card offers and exclusions (Amazon Pay ICICI, Flipkart Axis, Myntra Kotak, Tata Neu, SBI, HDFC)

### Takeaway
The headline co-brand cashbacks appear NOT to apply to gold. Amazon Pay ICICI excludes gold coins (reported from 11 Oct 2025); Flipkart Axis excludes Gold & Jewellery; Myntra Kotak was discontinued in July 2025. Tata Neu Infinity is unclear.

### Cited Findings
- Amazon Pay ICICI: several third-party reviews say no cashback on gold coins (physical and digital) on Amazon, with the exclusion expanded around 11 Oct 2025; one page claims one excluded item voids cashback on the whole order (single-source). Also, flights/hotels via Amazon Pay lost the 5%/3% rate in Jan 2026 — [Desidime](https://www.desidime.com/discussions/amazon-pay-icici-card-update-information), [BankBazaar](https://bb.bankbazaar.com/credit-card/amazon-pay-icici-credit-card.html), [GrabOn review 2026](https://www.grabon.in/indulge/finance/amazon-pay-icici-credit-card-review/), [CardExpress](https://cardexpress.in/reviews/icici-amazon). Not verified against ICICI's own terms.
- Flipkart Axis: from 20 June 2025, 5% on Flipkart/Cleartrip and 7.5% on Myntra, each capped at Rs 4,000 per statement quarter; excluded categories for cashback include Gold & Jewellery. Flipkart's own FAQ and T&C also list gold purchases as ineligible — [Axis cashback T&C PDF](https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/default-document-library/cashback-tncs---final.pdf), [Flipkart cashback T&C](https://flipkart.com/pages/cbc-cashback-product-tnc), [Flipkart FAQ](https://www.flipkart.com/pages/cbc-card-campaign-tnc2). These are official; the gold exclusion is consistent across versions.
- Myntra Kotak: discontinued from 10 July 2025; its 7.5% was an instant discount with a Rs 750 per-transaction cap. A deal thread mentions a Bhima 24KT coin on Myntra with the card, but eligibility was not answered — [Paisabazaar](https://www.paisabazaar.com/credit-card/kotak-myntra-discontinued-how-to-save-on-myntra/), [Desidime](https://www.desidime.com/deals/bhima-plain-flower-24kt-gold-coin). Myntra 7.5% now sits with Flipkart Axis (see above).
- Tata Neu Infinity (HDFC): Tata CLiQ's support page says NeuCoins are earned on all categories except gold coins and jewellery (page last modified April 2022) — [Tata Digital support](https://support.tatadigital.com/support/solutions/articles/81000395599-how-do-i-earn-neucoins-at-tata-cliq-). Comparison sites list Tanishq as a 5% category, and forum users report mixed results on jewellery — [Desidime](https://www.desidime.com/discussions/dilemma-should-we-use-hdfc-tata-neu-credit-card-at-tanishq-for-jewellery-purchase). A 2022 SmartBuy offer excluded jewellery and gold coins — [HDFC SmartBuy](https://offers.smartbuy.hdfcbank.com/offer_details/tata-neu-hdfc-bank-credit-card/21138/Z2Sklms%3D).
- SBI Card: PhonePe SBI Card Select Black notice effective 1 July 2026 adds jewellery to reward exclusions/caps (that card only; coins not named). A 2023 SBI Cashback card notice excludes MCC 5944 (jewellery). A community guide says SBI Elite and SimplyCLICK do not exclude MCC 5944, but MCCs vary by acquirer — [SBI Card PDF Apr 2026](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/ekit-tncs/PhonePe-Black-pdf-27Apr2026.pdf), [CardTrail](https://cardtrail.in/rewards/credit-card-category-exclusions-india/), [CardExpress MCC 5944](https://cardexpress.in/mcc-guide/code/5944)
- Tanishq offers: no current official Tanishq card offer found; a CashKaro coin cashback of up to 2% expired 31 Aug 2026; a 2021 Kotak Tanishq offer excluded gold/silver coins — [CashKaro](https://cashkaro.com/stores/tanishq-coupons), [Kotak T&C](https://images.kotak.com/bank/mailers/2021/files/TANISHQ-Festive-TnC.pdf)

### Inferences
- Plan on Amazon Pay ICICI and Flipkart Axis earning nothing on coins; for other cards expect base rate at best, possibly zero if the merchant MCC is jewellery (5944).
- Whether a coin from a Tata brand earns NeuCoins is unresolved; test with a small purchase or check the Tata Neu app.

### Gaps
- Amazon-specific, Myntra-specific and Tata CLiQ coin-listing offers (bank instant discounts) for 2026 not found. No ICICI primary T&C opened. No Axis Edge/ICICI iShop/SBI Rewards coin offers found. Rewards on e-commerce gold at the full card rate: not confirmed for any card.

## Tanishq voucher / Gyftr route and caps

### Takeaway
Buying Tanishq vouchers via HDFC SmartBuy is the commonly cited workaround, but caps and bonus-point limits changed in 2024-2026 and sources conflict. Whether a voucher can be redeemed for a gold coin was not found.

### Cited Findings
- HDFC Infinia/Infinia Metal Tanishq voucher redemption cap of 50,000 points: sources conflict between per calendar quarter (from Oct 2024) and per calendar month (HDFC terms PDF) — [Livefromalounge](https://livefromalounge.com/hdfc-bank-credit-cards-imposes-fresh-caps-on-earn-and-burn-of-points/), [HDFC Infinia T&C](https://www.paisabazaar.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/HDFC-Infinia-Credit-Card-Rewards-Program_TC.pdf)
- From 1 July 2026, bonus points on SmartBuy brand vouchers (Gyftr/Woohoo) are capped monthly; for Infinia reportedly 3,000 bonus points per month, about Rs 22,500 of voucher spend. Whether Tanishq is covered is not stated — [Cardtrail](https://cardtrail.in/rewards/maximize-hdfc-reward-points-2026/), [Angel One on Feb 2026 HDFC changes](https://oga-prod.angelone.in/news/stocks/hdfc-bank-credit-card-reward-points-revised-effective-february-1-2026)
- Jan 2026 article: Tanishq vouchers can be paid up to 70% with points — same Cardtrail link above.
- An Amazon Prime Rewards deal offered Tanishq gift-card cashback (min order Rs 19,999); date and current status unknown — [Desidime](https://www.desidime.com/deals/amazon-prime-rewards-gold-tanishq-gift-card-get-up-to-5000-back-3-offer-min-order-19999)

### Inferences
- Gift-card routes are an HDFC-premium-card tactic, not a low/mid-range one, and are exposed to cap changes.

### Gaps
- Current official SmartBuy Tanishq cap and earn rate; Axis Edge / other bank voucher routes; low/mid-range card equivalents.

## Digital gold with coin delivery (Jar, PhonePe, MMTC-PAMP, SafeGold) and SEBI warning

### Takeaway
SEBI warned on 8 Nov 2025 that digital gold is unregulated. Spreads and card-payment fees were not found.

### Cited Findings
- SEBI said digital gold products are neither securities nor commodity derivatives, sit outside its purview, and carry counterparty/operational risk — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/gold/sebi-warns-investors-against-digital-gold-products-issues-caution), [Groww](https://groww.in/blog/holding-digital-gold-sebi-issues-warning)
- Withdrawals from digital-gold fintechs rose nearly threefold after the warning; UPI digital gold purchases fell 47% in November 2025 — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/commodities/digital-gold-fintechs-face-scrutiny-amid-sebi-warning-125111101962_1.html), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/purchase-of-digital-gold-contracts-via-upi-contracts-47-in-november-125120800898_1.html)
- Karnataka CID searched Jar's premises; Jar says it is cooperating — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=550425)
- 3% GST applies to digital gold at purchase — [Vyapar](https://vyaparapp.in/blog/gst-on-gold/)

### Gaps
- Buy/sell spreads, card fees, coin-delivery charges, and credit-card eligibility for Jar/PhonePe/SafeGold: not found. Outlook Money gives the SEBI date as 2024 while other sources say 2025; 2025 is likely correct.

## Risks and net effective cost for a 10 g coin

### Takeaway
No reliable net comparison can be built from what was found; card cashback on coins is probably zero on the main co-brand cards, so cost is driven by making charge plus 3% GST.

### Cited Findings
- Tanishq 10 g: making about 7.5% of gold value plus 3% GST (snapshot, see first section). Amazon-listed Malabar/MMTC-PAMP/Bhima 10 g: Rs 1.63-1.69 lakh in June 2026 (affiliate list) — links above.
- Card-category exclusions vary by merchant MCC and acquirer, so the statement entry decides whether points post — [CardTrail](https://cardtrail.in/rewards/credit-card-category-exclusions-india/)

### Inferences
- Illustrative only: if a Tanishq coin has a 7.5% making charge and a card earns 0%, the effective cost is about 10.7% over metal value (1.075 x 1.03) versus the metal-only price; a lower-making-charge brand sold online saves the difference in making charge, if the seller is genuine.
- Counterfeit risk is greatest on marketplace third-party sellers; buy only "sold by" the brand or an authorised seller and insist on the invoice. (Inference; no counterfeit-specific source was found.)

### Gaps
- No counterfeit/fake-seller reports, coin return policies, or exchange-without-invoice rules found. No current dated price for same-weight coins at store vs online.
